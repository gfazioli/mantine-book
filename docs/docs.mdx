## Usage

`Curl` curls a single sheet of paper. It has two faces declared as
`<Curl.Front>` and `<Curl.Back>` children — grab any point of the sheet's
free edge and drag in any direction to fold it. The play-zone is twice the
sheet width: the sheet rests in the right half and sweeps toward the spine
(the centre) as it curls; once turned, it rests in the left half and folds
back the other way.

<Demo data={demos.usage} />

## Faces

A sheet has two faces, declared as compound children: `<Curl.Front>` (the
resting side) and `<Curl.Back>` (revealed as the page turns). Each face can
hold any React node — text, images, MDX, even a `<canvas>` — rendered once and
owned by React, so event handlers inside a face stay alive.

`Curl.Back` is optional: omit it and the back renders blank (filled with the
page background). Toggle it below:

<Demo data={demos.faces} />

## Content and alignment

Each face clips overflow and centers smaller content by default. Use the
`align` prop on `<Curl>` for both faces, or per-face on `<Curl.Front>` /
`<Curl.Back>` to override. It accepts `{ horizontal, vertical }`, each one of
`start` / `center` / `end`, and only affects content smaller than the face:

<Demo data={demos.align} />

### Media content

`img`, `svg`, `video` and `canvas` are automatically scaled to fit the face
(`object-fit: contain`), so oversized artwork is letterboxed rather than
cropped:

The default `flat` variant needs no canvas and works everywhere. If WebGL is
unavailable or a face snapshot fails, `rounded` falls back to `flat`
automatically, so it is always safe to opt in. Switch the variant and drag the
sheet to compare them:

### Curl radius

`curlRadius` (rounded only, in px, default ~0.32 × width) sets how tightly the
page wraps: a smaller radius makes a tight roll that turns the page sooner, a
larger one a gentle, loose curl. Drag the slider, then drag the sheet:

<Demo data={demos.curlRadius} />

## Dimensions

`width` and `height` size the sheet in CSS px. The interactive **play-zone is
twice the width**: the sheet rests in the right half and the curl sweeps left
toward the spine, so a `width={260}` sheet occupies a `520px`-wide box.

<Demo data={demos.dimensions} />

## Shadows and lighting

`shadowOpacity` (0–1) controls how strongly the lifting page is shaded as it
curves away from the light, and `shadowColor` (a `MantineColor` or any CSS
color) tints that shadow. Both apply to **either variant**.

On top of the shadow, the `rounded` variant also **lights its own 3D surface**:
a Lambert term darkens the curve, a tight specular ridge highlights the apex,
and the light automatically follows the direction you fold so the gloss reads
in both turn directions. This lighting needs no configuration — it is part of
the 3D render, which is why there is no separate lighting control.

<Demo data={demos.shadow} />

## Page background

`pageBackground` (a `MantineColor` or any CSS color) fills each face behind its
content, so transparent content sits on a solid sheet.

<Demo data={demos.pageBackground} />

**Reserved:** `revealBackground` is accepted for forward compatibility but has
no visible effect yet — it will paint the area uncovered by the curl once the
reveal layer ships in a future release.

## Interaction and release

When you release a drag the sheet settles with an animation over `flippingTime`
ms. `flipThreshold` (0–100, percent of a full turn) is the cutoff: drag past it
and the sheet completes the turn (showing the Back); below it, it settles back
to rest. `disabled` removes the drag interaction entirely.

<Demo data={demos.release} />

## Touch gestures

On touch devices `Curl` also recognizes a **swipe** — a quick flick that
completes the flip even when you release *below* `flipThreshold`. A gesture
counts as a swipe only when it travels at least `swipeDistance` px in under
`swipeTimeThreshold` ms, so raising `swipeDistance` (or lowering
`swipeTimeThreshold`) demands a longer / faster flick.

`mobileScrollSupport` (default `true`) keeps vertical page scrolling alive:
the component waits for a clearly horizontal gesture before claiming the touch,
so a vertical drag that starts on the sheet still scrolls the page. Set it to
`false` to capture every touch on the sheet immediately (page scroll from the
sheet is then blocked).

<Demo data={demos.gestures} />

## Events

`onFold` fires continuously during a drag and the release settle with
`{ progress, phase }` (`phase` is `'move'` or `'settle'`). `onFlip` fires once
when a settle finishes, with the resting `{ flipped }` state. Drag the sheet and
watch the readout:
