
You can import styles within a layer `@layer mantine-book` by importing `@gfazioli/mantine-book/styles.layer.css` file.

```tsx

```

## Usage

`Curl` curls a single sheet of paper. It has two faces declared as
`<Curl.Front>` and `<Curl.Back>` children — grab the right edge (top or
bottom) and drag toward the left to fold the sheet. The play-zone is twice
the sheet width: the sheet rests in the right half and sweeps left as it
curls. `Curl.Back` is optional — when omitted the back renders blank.

<Demo data={demos.usage} />

## Configurator

Tweak the sheet size, the settle duration, the completion threshold, and
the shadow intensity. Drag the right edge and release: past the threshold
the sheet settles open (showing the Back); below it, it settles back to rest.

<Demo data={demos.configurator} />

## Content alignment

Each face clips overflow and centers smaller content by default. Use the
`align` prop (on `<Curl>` or per-face on `<Curl.Front>` / `<Curl.Back>`) to
change placement. Images, SVG and video are scaled to fit (`object-fit:
contain`).
