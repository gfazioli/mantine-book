## Usage

`Curl` curls a single sheet of paper. It has two faces declared as
`<Curl.Front>` and `<Curl.Back>` children — grab any point of the sheet's
free edge and drag in any direction to fold it. The play-zone is twice the
sheet width: the sheet rests in the right half and sweeps toward the spine
(the centre) as it curls; once turned, it rests in the left half and folds
back the other way. `Curl.Back` is optional — when omitted the back renders
blank.

    <Table.Tr>
      <Table.Td>rounded</Table.Td>

      </Table.Td>
    </Table.Tr>
  </Table.Tbody>

The default `flat` variant needs no canvas and works everywhere. The
`rounded` variant lifts the page as a real curved surface, lit with a soft
specular ridge; `curlRadius` (px, default ~0.32 × width) controls how tightly
it wraps — smaller turns the page sooner, larger is a gentler curl. If WebGL
is unavailable or a face snapshot fails, `rounded` falls back to `flat`
automatically, so it is always safe to opt in.

Toggle the **Variant** control in the configurator below to compare them.

## Configurator

Tweak the sheet size, the settle duration, the completion threshold, the
shadow intensity, the curl radius, and the variant. Drag the free edge and
release: past the threshold the sheet settles open (showing the Back); below
it, it settles back to rest.

## Content alignment

Each face clips overflow and centers smaller content by default. Use the
`align` prop (on `<Curl>` or per-face on `<Curl.Front>` / `<Curl.Back>`) to
change placement. Images, SVG and video are scaled to fit (`object-fit:
contain`).
